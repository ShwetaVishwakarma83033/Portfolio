Customer Support

Power BI Challenge 42

AI vs. Human Support Analysis

Analyzed 12,000 tickets from January 2025 to September 2026 to see whether AI support improved efficiency while maintaining customer satisfaction. The analysis compared AI-only, AI-to-human, and human-only support paths.

AI-only CSAT
4.40
AI-only SLA attainment
95.6%
AI-only cost per ticket
€1.46

AI-to-human handoffs accounted for 29% of tickets and 58% of total costs. Late handoffs and email queue delays were key issues. Improved routing and processes could save an estimated €4K–€12K.

Method and key learning

I cleaned and validated the ticket data, identifying 514 misclassified SLA records. I built a star schema with five dimension tables using Power Query and DAX, then compared like-for-like tickets across CSAT, SLA attainment, resolution time, repeat contacts, escalations, and cost.

The two-page dashboard uses scorecards, heatmaps, treemaps, and driver analysis. Validating SLA classifications and comparing similar tickets helped avoid overstating AI’s impact.

Environment & Market Research

Power BI · DAX · SQL · Power Query

Market Research for Air Purifiers

Validated 230,000+ records across 200+ cities to explore where AirPure Innovations could prioritize market research. The dashboard combines air quality, health, EV registrations, air purifier sales, and population data. DataFul supplied AQI and health datasets for the challenge.

Dashboard pages and findings
  • Air quality. AQI was generally worse in winter and post-harvest months. Delhi showed limited weekend improvement, suggesting that further analysis should examine sources beyond weekday traffic.
  • Health. The dashboard compares PM2.5 and PM10 readings with reported health indicators and treatment costs. These comparisons show associations.
  • EV adoption. Delhi and Chandigarh illustrate how higher EV adoption can coexist with poor air quality.
  • Market demand. Search interest for air purifiers rose during severe AQI episodes. Sales and brand comparisons helped identify potential gaps in the market.
  • City priorities. The dashboard’s risk score highlighted Byrnihat, Delhi, and Hajipur for further review.

In Power BI Desktop, open the project’s .pbix file and use the slicers to explore dates, locations, pollutants, and disease categories.

Logistics & Supply Chain

Python · Google OR-Tools · TSPLIB

Vehicle Routing Optimization

Built a capacitated vehicle routing model for a mid-mile logistics scenario inspired by Delhivery. The TSPLIB A-n32-k5 benchmark has one depot, 31 customers, and five vehicles with 100 units of capacity each.

Fleet capacity used
82%
Vehicles used
5
Total route distance
784 units
Method and operational findings

I parsed the benchmark coordinates and customer demand, built a Euclidean distance matrix, and used OR-Tools with Guided Local Search to find routes within vehicle capacity limits. I used Pandas and NumPy for data preparation and Matplotlib to visualize the routes.

The five routes served all 31 customers and carried 410 of 500 available units. Vehicle loads ranged from 44 to 98 units, with three vehicles close to capacity. The route map gives planners a starting point for evaluating customer clusters and potential micro-hub locations.

Logistics & Supply Chain

FP20 Analytics Challenge 25 · Power BI · DAX

Inventory Management Analysis

Built an interactive Power BI report to identify products that need restocking, monitor supplier lead times, and compare inventory across categories, countries, and warehouses.

Products analyzed
5,000
Below reorder point
262
Total inventory value
₹1.25B
Dashboard findings and recommendations
  • Stock overview. The dataset contains 3 million units across 5,000 products. Of those products, 774 were out of stock. Clothing was the most stocked category, and Germany, Spain, and Belgium held the most stock.
  • Supplier performance. Average lead time was 14.96 days. SUP041 had the longest lead time at 17.3 days. Out-of-stock products averaged 222.38 days since their last restock. Restocking peaked in March, May, and October.
  • Warehouse and turnover. Two shelf locations held 13 SKUs each, the highest observed count. Sports and Clothing had the fastest turnover.

The two dashboard pages use DAX measures, drill-downs, and filters to explore stock levels, pricing, supplier performance, and warehouse load. I recommended prioritizing the 262 products below reorder point, monitoring suppliers with long lead times, rebalancing stock across countries, and promoting or rotating high-value, slow-moving inventory.
